Wisteria Rules

Simple rules to keep Wisteria respectful, competitive, and enjoyable.

Community Guidelines

Rule 1

Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Guidelines.

https://discord.com/terms - https://discord.com/guidelines

Rule 2

Do not harass anyone. Treat everyone in the group with respect.

Rule 3

Do not organize, promote, or participate in hate speech or hateful conduct based on attributes such as their race, ethnicity, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religious affiliation, disabilities, or other protected classifications.

Rule 4

Do not make threats of violence or threaten to harm others. This includes indirect or suggestive threats, as well as doxxing.

Rule 5

Do not share media depicting NSFW of any kind, including nudity, overly suggestive content, gore, excessive violence, or animal harm.

Rule 6

Do not spam or send text walls of any kind, including copy pastas, repeated text, repeated commands, rapidly switching voice channels or repeated mentions of other users without a valid reason.

Rule 7

Do not discuss sensitive topics such as politics, world events, religion, etc.

Rule 8

Do not advertise other groups or servers unless someone asks you to.

Rule 9

Do not misuse any channels, information about each channel is listed in the channel topic which can be found on top of the channel.
