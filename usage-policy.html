Usage Policy

Use Wisteria services respectfully, legally, and only for the group.

You May

  • Use public pages and private resources you are allowed to use.
  • Report security problems, mistakes, abuse, or exposed information to a group admin.
  • Use the dashboard only within the permissions on your Discord account.

You Must Not

  • Try to bypass sign-in, rate limits, permissions, or other security controls.
  • Probe, scan, disrupt, overload, or access Wisteria systems or accounts without permission.
  • Share private documents, login details, dashboard data, or access links without permission.
  • Upload malware, misleading material, unlawful content, or unrelated files.
  • Use admin tools to harass, impersonate, or unfairly target members.

Enforcement

Wisteria may remove content, remove access, keep security records, and report serious abuse when needed.
