Who We Are

Wisteria is a Minecraft geopolitics group built around nations, diplomacy, strategy, and shared stories.

About Wisteria

Wisteria is a group of players who take part in Minecraft geopolitical projects. We build nations, make alliances, and work through diplomacy and strategy together.

We are not one server. We are a group that moves between worlds and creates new stories together.

Our Community

People can take many roles. Some lead and handle diplomacy. Others plan, build, gather, or organise.

Leadership

Lead people and help guide Wisteria.

Strategy

Make alliances, plan, negotiate, and make big choices.

Creativity

Build nations and create stories together.

Our Goals

  • • Make fun geopolitical projects
  • • Build leadership and teamwork
  • • Give people ways to help
  • • Keep the group active and welcoming
  • • Make stories across different worlds

What Makes Us Different?

Wisteria is about the people behind the nations. Every project is shaped by the group, through leadership, diplomacy, teamwork, and strategy.
