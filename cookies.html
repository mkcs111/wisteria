Cookie Policy

Wisteria uses only essential cookies needed to run secure sign-in and security protections. It does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Authentication Session Cookie

When you sign in, Wisteria uses a secure, HTTP-only, same-site session cookie to keep the authentication session active. The cookie does not store your password or Discord access token. Server-side controls expire, rotate, and revoke dashboard sessions.

Security Token

A session-bound token protects forms from cross-site request forgery. It is used only to verify that state-changing requests came from the signed-in browser.

Cookie Notice Acknowledgement

After you acknowledge the Terms before dashboard login, Wisteria stores a small functional cookie recording that acknowledgement. It has no tracking purpose and is sent only to Wisteria over HTTPS.

Your Choices

You can clear cookies in your browser at any time. Clearing essential cookies signs you out and may require you to acknowledge the Terms again before using the dashboard. Blocking essential cookies prevents authenticated areas from working safely.
